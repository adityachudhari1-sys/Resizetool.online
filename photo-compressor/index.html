Compress a Photo Online

A photo has both pixel dimensions and file size. Check whether the receiving form specifies one or both before compressing.

🔒 Selected files are processed in your browser. File contents are not sent to a ResizeTool upload endpoint.

Open the compress a photo online tool

The tool opens on the main ResizeTool page. Choose a file there to begin.

How to compress a photo online

  1. Check the form’s accepted format, maximum KB, and pixel dimensions.
  2. Select your photo and enter a target KB size, if one is specified.
  3. Download the result and confirm it remains clear and below the upload limit.

Questions and answers

Can this meet every application requirement?

It can target JPEG file size, but it does not verify dimensions, background, lighting, or any organization-specific photo rules.

What if the target is not reached?

Some photos cannot be encoded below a requested size at usable quality. Reduce dimensions if allowed or choose a less strict target.

Is the photo sent to the server?

The selected photo is processed in your browser and is not sent to a ResizeTool upload endpoint.
