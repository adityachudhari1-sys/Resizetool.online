Convert PNG to JPG

JPG is commonly accepted for photos and may produce a smaller file. Transparent areas cannot remain transparent in a JPG.

🔒 Selected files are processed in your browser. File contents are not sent to a ResizeTool upload endpoint.

Open the convert png to jpg tool

The tool opens on the main ResizeTool page. Choose a file there to begin.

How to convert png to jpg

  1. Select the PNG image.
  2. Choose JPG in the Convert Image tool.
  3. Convert and download the JPEG; inspect the background and check the destination’s format rules.

Questions and answers

What happens to transparent pixels?

JPG has no transparency channel. Transparent areas are rendered against the browser canvas background.

Will JPG always be smaller?

No. Result size depends on image dimensions, detail, and JPEG quality.

Can I convert the JPG back to a transparent PNG later?

Changing formats cannot restore transparency removed during conversion. Keep the original PNG if transparency matters.
