Convert JPG to PNG

PNG is useful for graphics and workflows that need lossless encoding, but converting a JPEG cannot restore detail already lost in the JPG.

🔒 Selected files are processed in your browser. File contents are not sent to a ResizeTool upload endpoint.

Open the convert jpg to png tool

The tool opens on the main ResizeTool page. Choose a file there to begin.

How to convert jpg to png

  1. Select the JPG image.
  2. Choose PNG in the Convert Image tool.
  3. Convert, download the PNG, and confirm the receiving service accepts that format.

Questions and answers

Does JPG to PNG restore original image quality?

No. PNG output avoids additional lossy JPEG encoding, but it cannot recover detail already discarded in the source JPG.

Will the PNG be smaller than the JPG?

Not necessarily. PNG files can be larger, especially for photographs.

Can JPG transparency be recovered?

No. JPEG does not store transparency, so conversion cannot recreate a transparent background.
